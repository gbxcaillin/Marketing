Critical gaps
Offer Creation & Positioning
Missing
The entire summit assumes the offer already exists. Every session teaches how to present, advertise, and convert for an offer — but none covers how to build one. Pricing, packaging, guarantees, bonuses, competitive positioning.
TaughtHow to frame the offer in ad copy (hooks, CTAs)
MissingHow to construct the offer itself, test pricing, build guarantees
Budget & Unit Economics
Missing
The 3-phase process says "increase budgets on proven campaigns" but never explains how to know what you can afford per lead. No LTV:CAC calculation, no CPA target-setting, no budget allocation model.
TaughtWhen to scale (Phase 3), general principle of spending more on winners
MissingHow to calculate target CPA, set daily budgets, model LTV:CAC ratios
Campaign Structure & Account Setup
Missing
Andromeda explains what to put inside campaigns. Nothing explains how to structure the account: CBO vs ABO, naming conventions, how many campaigns to run, when to use Advantage+ vs manual, ad set organisation.

MissingAccount architecture, campaign types, budget optimisation settings

Well covered by the summit

Strong
Thorough coverage across multiple sessions: avatar speedrun, towards/away motivators, Green Brain vs Red Brain, tribalism, neuromarketing (50/30/20 decision split), emotional messaging framework, mirroring language.
Ad Copywriting (Meta & Google)
Strong
Two dedicated sessions plus principles woven through others. Rule of One, hook writing (imply, exclude, curiosity), body proof tools, CTA formula, Google headline/description structure, emotional/logical/emotional framework.
Content & Creative Strategy
Strong
Camouflaging ads, 3-step video script framework, testimonial 2.0, image vs video, competitor research via Ad Library, creative diversity roadmap with messaging pillars, Andromeda algorithm mechanics.
Landing Page Design Principles
Strong
Momentum principle, remove doubt vs increase desire, mobile-first, consistent branding, split by service/audience, FAQ placement, website vs LP distinction. Clear philosophy even if the copywriting formula is light.
Conversions & Funnel Mechanics
Strong
Physical vs psychological resistance, one-step vs two-step funnels, speed-to-lead, booking rules (3-day window, 15-min slots), reminder cadence, quality vs quantity seesaw, Microsoft Clarity.
Nurture Sequences
Strong
Three distinct types (notificational, lead, sales) with clear jobs. GHL logic branching, custom values, awareness spectrum positioning. Conceptually complete — email-specific execution is the gap.
A/B Testing (All Platforms)
Strong
Three dedicated sessions covering GHL split testing, Meta Experiments, and Google Experiments. Consistent principles: one variable, statistical significance, 30-day minimum, what to test vs what not to test.
Show Rates & Sales Operations
Strong
70% target, morning booking strategy, rep hiring via lead ads, mock call + ethics screening, scaling playbook (fill one calendar first). Practical and immediately actionable.
Lead Quality Tracking
Strong
UTM parameters, lead tracker spreadsheet, AI call transcript review, staged review cadence (weekly then biweekly). Closes the loop between ads and revenue — the dashboard/reporting layer is the gap.
3-Phase Launch Framework
Strong
Clear 90-day structure: data gathering (0-30), optimisation (30-60), scaling (60-90+). Platform-specific actions at each phase. Gives beginners a timeline they can follow without guessing.
Marketing Psychology
Strong
Naive realism, constructing sufficient reasons, skepticism ads, Reddit testing, negativity bias, "join the conversation in their head." Woven through multiple sessions rather than isolated — which makes it stick.
Organic Content
Strong
3 E's framework, frequency guidance, reuse-as-ads strategy, brand vs performance distinction, channel focus. Light but complete enough — organic isn't the summit's primary scope.

Where this leaves you

The summit is strongest on the creative and strategic side of the pipeline — knowing your customer, writing compelling copy, and building content that doesn't look like an ad. It's weakest on the operational and financial side — structuring accounts, setting budgets, building the offer itself, and what happens after the sale. The biggest single gap is offer creation: every other skill amplifies an offer that already works, but none of them can save an offer that doesn't. The second biggest is budget and unit economics: without knowing your CPA target, you can't tell whether a campaign is winning or losing money.
