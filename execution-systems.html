01

Competitor Intelligence

Fills Zeely's biggest gap. Modelled on Tiarna's competitor research step and Brandon's Reddit test, with runtime as the evidence standard.
Creative DiversityNeuromarketingOffer Positioning
Trigger
New product, offer, or market. Quarterly refresh otherwise.
Inputs
Product URL or one-paragraph description. Up to 5 known competitor names (optional).
Owner
Media buyer. 2 hours.

Steps

  1. List competitors that are actively advertising right now. A brand that isn't running ads teaches nothing about ads. Tiarna: Meta Ad Library is the manual route.
  2. Pull each competitor's active ads sorted by how long they've run. An ad at 60+ days is paying for itself; treat it as a proven concept.
  3. Log every long-runner in the swipe file: hook type, format (static / UGC / talking-head / carousel / testimonial), offer, CTA, landing page type, and the Away From & Towards motivator it leans on.
  4. Extract the 3 patterns that recur across competitors. These are the brief's proven angles.
  5. Note what nobody is saying. Pick 2 as differentiation angles. Sanity-check the boldest one with the Reddit test: post the hook anonymously and read the raw reaction before spending on it.
Connector hook
With AdWhispr connected: save_my_brand → find_competitors (use only its verified advertisers) → get_brand_ads with sortBy: longevity → generate_brief. Without it: Meta Ad Library, filter to active, record the same fields by hand.

Decision rules

  • 30+ days of runtime or it isn't evidence.
  • 3 verified advertisers minimum before you call anything a pattern. Fewer than 3? Widen to the adjacent niche.
  • Never brainstorm competitor names from memory. Verified advertisers only.
  • Swipe the angle, never the ad. Adapt to the niche per Tiarna.

Outputs

  • Swipe file (table, one row per long-running ad)
  • Pattern summary (3 lines)
  • 3 proven angles + 2 differentiation angles → into the Product Brief
02

Product Intake

Zeely's Generate from Link: paste a URL, get title, images, price, description. Here the brief also carries the avatar, the awareness level, and the offer stack, so every later system reads from one page.
Customer AvatarOffer Creation
Trigger
Any new product, offer, or campaign.
Inputs
Product/offer URL or manual entry. System 01 angles.
Owner
Strategist. 45 minutes.

Steps

  1. Extract the basics: name, one-line description, price, 3–6 images, primary CTA.
  2. Run the Avatar Speedrun: what they're running away from, what they're running towards, and which tribe they want to join or leave.
  3. Place them on the awareness spectrum (unaware → problem → solution → product → most aware). This decides how much the ad has to explain.
  4. Write the Green Brain Roadmap line: what it is → the result it delivers → the emotional effect of that result. This becomes the headline seed for every asset.
  5. Choose Curious vs Committed language based on sales capacity. Committed ("how my clients grow revenue") pulls hotter, fewer leads.
  6. Attach the offer stack: core outcome, vehicle, bonuses, guarantee, price anchor. The summit assumes the offer exists; the supplement's Module 01 builds it.
  7. Set one conversion goal (traffic, leads, purchase) and flag any compliance category.

Decision rules

  • One product per brief. Multi-product URL → multiple briefs.
  • No price on the page → the goal is leads, not purchase.
  • Can't write the Green Brain line in one sentence → the offer isn't clear yet. Stop and finish Module 01.
  • Awareness level unknown → default to problem-aware; most Meta cold traffic sits there.

Outputs

  • One-page Product Brief (template below)
  • Named by product; every downstream asset references it
03

Creative Factory

Zeely's Creative Builder, Batch Mode (up to 20 statics per run) and Brand-Inspired Remix. Concepts come from Tiarna's and Brandon's frameworks so the output doesn't read as generic AI.
Ad Content CreationCreative DiversityVideo & Image Production
Trigger
A completed Product Brief.
Inputs
Brief; swipe file; brand kit (fonts, colours, no-logo rule); testimonial footage if any.
Owner
Creative lead. One batch per week.

Steps

  1. Run the Creative Diversity Roadmap: audit what's live (how many truly unique concepts?), segment the brief's angles into messaging pillars (speed, trust, results, price…), then write 6–10 concepts per pillar. Each concept has its own angle, script, visual, and copy.
  2. Apply Camouflaging Ads to every concept: no logo, no brand intro, no "Hi I'm X from Y". Open with the problem or the promise.
  3. Batch statics. Produce 10–20 per run, each in feed 1:1, story 9:16, landscape 1.91:1. Prefer carousels where a story needs several beats: image simplicity with video's stopping power.
  4. Remix 2–3 long-runners from the swipe file with your product and voice. Angle, not artwork.
  5. Video scripts follow Tiarna's 3-step script: stop the right person scrolling → prove your point → tell them what to do next. Cold: 15–30s. Warm: 30–60s.
  6. Every batch includes one Testimonial 2.0 piece (the 8-question interview, customer does the convincing) and one skepticism ad ("you're probably thinking this won't work for you…").
  7. Production is a partial gap: scripting is taught, filming and editing aren't. Use the supplement's production stack (phone + ring light + CapCut) or the connector below.
Connector hook
With OpenArt connected: openart_generate_image for static concepts and openart_generate_video for image-to-video and avatar-style clips, briefed with the concept sheet line by line. Without it: Canva for statics, CapCut for edits, customer-recorded testimonials for video.

Decision rules

  • 10–15 unique ads per ad set at launch. The summit's floor, not Zeely's 1–3.
  • No two creatives share a hook. "Change the first sentence" and "swap the first 3 seconds" don't count as new concepts.
  • Logo in the first frame → rejected at review.
  • Every batch: ≥1 testimonial, ≥1 skepticism, ≥1 direct offer, ≥3 formats.
  • Silent-safe: every video reads with captions and no sound.

Outputs

  • Concept sheet (pillar → concept → hook → format)
  • Creative Library rows: concept, format, hook, angle, sizes, status (draft / approved / live / retired)
04

Copy Variants

Zeely generates captions, headlines and primary text in sets. This runs Brandon's copy frameworks to do the same for Meta and Google, so voice stays yours.
CopywritingLanding Page Copy
Trigger
A creative reaches "approved" in the library.
Inputs
Brief (Green Brain line, angles, language mode); the creative's hook.
Owner
Copywriter. 30 minutes per creative.

Steps

  1. Pick one of Brandon's three interchangeable structures (Hook/Story/Offer, Hook/Body/CTA, Emotional/Logical/Emotional) and hold the Rule of One: one idea, one promise, one emotion, one CTA.
  2. Write 5 hooks using the Writing Hooks rules: imply who it's for, imply who it's not for, open a loop you don't close.
  3. Write 3 primary texts (short under 125 chars; medium with a line break after the hook; long story-led) using at least two of the 6 proof tools: urgency, scarcity, social proof, authority, familiar language, benefits over features.
  4. Write 3 Meta headlines at ≤28 characters in Green Brain language, and 3 CTAs on the CTA formula: action + emotional benefit.
  5. For Google, build the RSA on the summit's headline structure: H1 ×2–4 = service name; H2 ×2–4 = shortened hook/motivator; H3 ×2–4 = proof or offer; 2 descriptions.
  6. Send every line through System 06 before it enters the library.

Decision rules

  • Never one variant. Each creative ships with ≥2 primary texts and ≥2 headlines.
  • Language mode comes from the brief: Committed when the sales team is at capacity, Curious when it needs volume.
  • "Click here to learn more" is banned as a CTA.
  • A hook that names the audience explicitly ("Attention Perth homeowners") gets rewritten as implied ("Sick of dodgy tradies?").

Outputs

  • Copy set attached to each library row: 5 hooks, 3 primary texts, 3 headlines, 3 CTAs
  • Google RSA set per brief
05

Campaign Launch

Zeely's one-screen launch: goal, location, age/gender, and a budget it calculates for you. Here the budget comes from unit economics, the structure from Ciara's data hierarchy, and the timeline from the 3-Phase Ad Process.
Campaign StructureAd TargetingGoogle SearchBudget & Unit Economics
Trigger
10+ approved creatives with copy for one brief, and the sales page (07) live.
Inputs
Brief; max allowable CPL / breakeven ROAS; audience lists by data tier.
Owner
Media buyer. Launch day + 72h hands-off.

Steps

  1. Objective = the brief's goal. Conversion events per Brandon: one-step funnel tracks Leads; two-step tracks Leads + Schedules.
  2. Budget from the backwards budget (supplement Module 02): revenue target → sales → calls → bookings → leads → daily spend = leads/day × max CPL. Floor: enough for ~50 conversion events a week per ad set.
  3. Structure per the 3-layer hierarchy and naming convention (Module 03): one campaign per objective; ad sets split by Ciara's data hierarchy: cold (1% lookalike seeded from closed sales, plus broad), warm (page viewers, engagers), hot (form fills, bookings that didn't show). Different message per retargeting layer.
  4. Phase 1 settings (Days 0–30, Data Gathering): broad targeting, 10–15 creatives, automatic placements. You're buying information, not optimising.
  5. Google: run the brand campaign regardless (exact + phrase only, low budget, high intent); competitors bidding on your name → immediately, otherwise minimal insurance budget. Non-brand search per Module 03's four campaign types.
  6. Pre-launch checklist: pixel fires, page loads on mobile under 3s, every link tested, compliance passed, UTMs on every link (source, medium, campaign, content).
  7. Submit. Log the tracker row with the hypothesis: "pain-hook pillar will beat $X CPL."
Connector hook
With AdWhispr: launch_meta_ad / launch_campaign for Meta, launch_search_campaign or launch_pmax_campaign for Google, launch_tiktok_campaign to go past Zeely's Meta-only ceiling; update_budget, pause_campaign, resume_campaign for management. Without it: Ads Manager and Google Ads with the same settings.

Decision rules

  • 72 hours no-touch after launch. Learning phase is not a signal.
  • Keep every ad running in Phase 1. Budget concentrates on 1–2 winners naturally; never judge an ad set by its weakest ad (Brandon).
  • Scale winners ≤20% every 3–5 days. Faster → duplicate at the higher budget instead.
  • Kill: 2× target CPA with zero conversions after 3 days.
  • Phase 2 (day 30) opens optimisation; Phase 3 (day 60) opens lookalikes and new platforms. Not before.
  • Under $20K/month spend, don't buy advanced tracking. The CRM is the source of truth (Brandon).

Outputs

  • Live campaign in the naming convention
  • Tracker row: launch date, budget, hypothesis, phase
  • Audience lists saved per data tier
06

Compliance Gate

Zeely's support team reviews every campaign against Meta's rules and checks links before it goes live. Make that a gate every asset passes, not a favour. The summit doesn't teach this; the supplement's Module 06 does.
Ad Policy & Compliance
Trigger
Any creative or copy moving to "approved".
Inputs
The asset; the five rejection triggers; the destination page.
Owner
Whoever didn't write it. 10 minutes.

Steps

  1. Scan for personal-attribute callouts: "you" + a personal problem in the same sentence. The summit's implied hooks are powerful here; keep them implied about the situation, not the person.
  2. Scan for before/after implications in imagery and testimonial cuts. Testimonial 2.0 step 6 ("after state") is fine as story; not as a promised result.
  3. Scan for income, earnings, or specific-result claims without substantiation.
  4. Scan for sensational or fear-based phrasing. Skepticism ads pass; "your business is dying" doesn't.
  5. Confirm the landing page delivers what the ad promises; free guide in the ad → no card wall on the page.
  6. Check restricted categories (health, finance, housing, employment). Confirm the account has a backup ad account in the same Business Manager.

Decision rules

  • Any flag → back to 03/04 with the line quoted and a compliant rewrite suggested. Nothing goes live with an open flag.
  • Rejected by the platform → request manual review first, edit second.
  • New ad account → first 2 weeks run only the plainest compliant ads to build history.
  • The writer never gates their own copy.

Outputs

  • Pass/fail per asset with flagged lines and rewrites
  • Account health log (rejections, appeals, outcomes)
07

Sales Page Generator

Zeely builds an AI sales page with Stripe/PayPal for businesses with no store. Here the page is specced from the brief using Brandon's landing page rules and booking rules, so it converts to shown calls, not just clicks.
Conversion FunnelsLanding Page OptimisationLanding Page CopyShow Rates
Trigger
A brief with no matching page. Built before 05 so tracking exists at launch.
Inputs
Brief; offer stack; testimonial footage; calendar or payment provider.
Owner
Funnel builder in GHL. 1 day.

Steps

  1. Choose the funnel: one-step (form → call) or two-step (form → booking → call). Two-step when lead quality matters more than volume (the Quality vs Quantity seesaw).
  2. Above the fold on the Momentum principle: Green Brain headline → Red Brain sub-headline → CTA. One hero (VSL or image). One trust element.
  3. Body on Remove doubt > increase desire: they already clicked, so eliminate reasons to leave. FAQs mid-page, not buried. Images below the VSL to cut cognitive load.
  4. Offer block with the value stack (Module 01): line items with values, total, price, guarantee directly beneath.
  5. Proof block cut from Testimonial 2.0 interviews. Consistent branding with the ad: colour, tone, imagery match or trust breaks.
  6. Booking per the Attendance & Booking Rules: today / tomorrow / day-after only; 15-minute slots; morning slots first (book calls before lunch). Thank-you page with a next step.
  7. Wire the Notificational Nurture: confirmation → 24h → 1h → 30min reminders. Install Microsoft Clarity for session recordings.

Decision rules

  • One page per offer per audience (Brandon: split LPs). No "all services" page.
  • Mobile designed first; desktop adapted.
  • Load time >3s → doesn't launch. Speed kills sales.
  • Reduce text ruthlessly: if a section doesn't remove a doubt, cut it.
  • A website is not a landing page. Ads never land on the homepage.

Outputs

  • Page spec, section by section, with copy
  • Live GHL page with pixel, UTM capture, calendar, reminder workflow
  • Clarity recording enabled

Performance Loop

Zeely tracks seven metrics, tells you to check daily for three days then two or three times a week, sort by ROAS and act on one or two suggestions. Same cadence, plus Patsy's lead tracker as the source of truth and thresholds Zeely leaves out.
Reporting & KPIsLead Quality TrackingA/B TestingRetargeting
Trigger
Any live campaign.
Inputs
Platform metrics; lead tracker; CRM outcomes; unit economics targets.
Owner
Media buyer daily, whole team Mondays.

Steps

  1. Keep Patsy's lead tracker live: every lead from click → form → booking → show → close, tagged by UTM. This, not the ad dashboard, says what's working.
  2. Days 1–3: one look per day, no changes. Days 4–7: one look, kill rules only. Week 2+: two or three looks a week.
  3. Every Monday: pull the week into the three-view dashboard (Module 05: daily snapshot, weekly trend, monthly P&L). Flag any metric that moved more than 20%. Decide. Execute by Friday. Log the change and its hypothesis.
  4. Diagnose top-down with the threshold table below: CTR fine but CPL bad → the page or offer, not the ad.
  5. Review lead quality weekly for the first 60 days, then biweekly. Run Patsy's AI call transcript review monthly: what do the closers say that the tyre-kickers don't? Feed it back into System 04.
  6. Test on the summit's rules: GHL pages at a 50/50 split to significance (Sophie); Meta experiments one variable at a time with the minimum runtime (Ciara); Google experiments 30 days minimum.
  7. Frequency past 3 or CTR down 30% from peak → creative refresh request to System 03.
Connector hook
With AdWhispr: get_account_performance and list_campaigns for the Monday pull; update_budget and pause_campaign for the actions. Without it: platform exports into the three-tab Google Sheet.

Decision rules

  • Max two changes per session. Sort by CPL (lead gen) or ROAS (sales), act on the top one or two.
  • Show rate under 70% → the leak is in nurture or booking, not the ads. Diagnose before scaling (Brandon).
  • Tracking is not truth post-iOS 14. The CRM number wins every disagreement.
  • No test is called before significance, and never on fewer than ~100 conversions per variant.
  • Never optimise for cheaper leads that don't close. Cost per qualified lead is the number.

Outputs

  • Monday dashboard row
  • Change log with hypothesis and result
  • Creative refresh and copy refinement requests
09

Organic Track

Zeely's Viral campaigns schedule AI videos as Reels. Here Raj's organic system runs alongside paid: it pre-tests hooks, builds warm audiences, and turns proven posts into ads with the engagement showing.
Ad Content CreationRetargeting
Trigger
Every approved video in the library; standing weekly slot.
Inputs
Creative Library; the 1–2 channels the audience actually lives on.
Owner
Content lead. 2 hours a week.

Steps

  1. Pick 1–2 channels and go deep (Raj: channel adoption). Don't spread.
  2. Post 1–2 times a week. Every post passes the 3 E's: educate, entertain, engage. The best do two.
  3. Strip the offer-heavy CTA off paid creatives before posting them organically. Post each video 3–5 days before its paid run.
  4. Log 24-hour organic metrics: views, watch-through, saves, shares.
  5. Reuse organic as ads: anything in the top 20% for watch-through jumps the paid queue, with the engagement visible.
  6. Build engager audiences from organic viewers; they're the lowest tier of Ciara's data hierarchy, but they're free.

Decision rules

  • Consistency beats volume. Miss a week → post the backlog, don't double up.
  • Top-20% watch-through → paid within 7 days.
  • Brand (organic) compounds; performance (paid) converts. Neither replaces the other. Both stay funded.

Outputs

  • Organic calendar
  • Organic-to-paid promotion list
  • Engager audience (refreshed weekly)

Downstream systems already in the playbook

These run after a lead exists and are already fully specified by the summit. Wire them once; the nine systems above feed them.

Nurture (Team)Three sequences with distinct jobs: Notificational (transactional), Lead Nurture (emotional, booking-focused, GHL logic branching and custom values), Sales Nurture (logical, case studies and ROI for booked leads). Position each on the awareness spectrum. Email infrastructure is a gap: authenticate the domain and warm it (Module 04) before any sequence goes live.
Show rates & reps (Brandon)Target 70% show rate. Morning slots. Hire reps through Meta lead ads; interview with mock calls and ethics tests. Scaling playbook when capacity, not leads, is the constraint.
Post-sale (gap)The summit's funnel ends at the sale. Supplement Module 07 adds the 7-day onboarding, the 3-trigger referral ask, review generation cut from Testimonial 2.0 interviews, and the entry → core → premium upsell path. Referrals are the only leads with a zero CPL; they belong in the tracker too.
