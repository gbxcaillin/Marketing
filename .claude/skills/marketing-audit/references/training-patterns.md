# Training Module Patterns

When building training supplement modules for missing or partial skills, use these content patterns. Each pattern gives you the core framework to teach and the key topics to cover.

Adapt everything to the company's context — their business model, language, and industry. These are starting frameworks, not scripts.

## Module Structure (every module follows this)

1. **Module header** — number, title, one-sentence lead
2. **Why this matters** — 1-2 paragraphs connecting the gap to the pipeline. What breaks without this skill? Why can't the adjacent skills compensate?
3. **Core framework** — the named, structured model. Give it a memorable name. Break it into numbered steps or components. This is the thing they'll remember.
4. **Practical detail** — formulas, tables, checklists, examples. The operational knowledge needed to actually implement the framework.
5. **Common mistakes** — 2-3 specific pitfalls. Draw these from the gap itself: what do people typically get wrong when this skill is self-taught?
6. **Exercise** — a checklist-style task they can complete with their own business data. Concrete, not theoretical.

## Content Patterns by Skill

### Customer Avatar & Psychographics
- **Framework:** Layered avatar (demographics → psychographics → awareness level → buying triggers)
- **Key content:** Fear/desire mapping, awareness spectrum (Schwartz's 5 levels), objection inventory, "day in the life" narrative, identity-level messaging
- **Exercise:** Build a complete avatar with at least 5 psychographic attributes

### Ad Content Creation
- **Framework:** Hook/Story/Offer structure, 3-step video script, Testimonial 2.0 (8-step)
- **Key content:** Multiple ad formats (video, image, carousel, UGC), hook formulas, content repurposing from one long-form to multiple short-form
- **Exercise:** Script 3 video ads using 3 different hook types

### Copywriting (Meta & Google)
- **Framework:** Platform-specific copy structures (Meta primary text + headline, Google RSA 15/4 structure)
- **Key content:** Character limits per platform, headline formulas, CTA variations, Rule of One, responsive search ad best practices
- **Exercise:** Write 5 headlines and 3 descriptions for a single offer, one set for Meta and one for Google

### Creative Diversity & Testing
- **Framework:** The creative diversity engine (quantity × variety × refresh cadence)
- **Key content:** Why algorithms reward diversity (Meta's Andromeda), how many variations to run (3-5 per ad set), refresh triggers (frequency > 3, CTR decay), static vs video vs carousel mix
- **Exercise:** Audit current creative — count formats, hooks, and visual styles in rotation

### Neuromarketing & Persuasion
- **Framework:** The resistance model (physical resistance → reduce friction; psychological resistance → build desire)
- **Key content:** Key biases (loss aversion, anchoring, social proof, authority, scarcity), emotional vs logical brain targeting, awareness-matched messaging
- **Exercise:** Map 3 psychological principles to specific points in your current funnel

### Ad Targeting & Audiences
- **Framework:** Data hierarchy (customers > leads > video viewers > engagers > interests)
- **Key content:** Custom audience types, lookalike percentages and when to use each, exclusion strategy, audience sizing, cold/warm/hot segmentation for campaign structure
- **Exercise:** Build audience lists for each tier of the data hierarchy using your existing data

### Campaign Structure & Account Setup
- **Framework:** The 3-layer hierarchy (campaign → ad set → ad) with the 70/20/10 budget rule
- **Key content:** Naming conventions (objective-offer-budget-date), CBO vs ABO decision tree, campaign types per platform, scaling structure (vertical vs horizontal)
- **Exercise:** Draw your campaign structure and apply the naming convention

### Google Search Campaigns
- **Framework:** Four campaign types (brand, competitor, non-brand, long-tail)
- **Key content:** Keyword match types (broad, phrase, exact), negative keyword strategy, quality score components (relevance, landing page, expected CTR), search terms report review cadence, Performance Max overview
- **Exercise:** Build a keyword list of 20 terms split across the four campaign types

### Retargeting
- **Framework:** Sequential retargeting (what to show at 1/7/14/30 days post-visit)
- **Key content:** Audience windows, frequency capping, creative strategy for warm vs hot audiences, cross-platform retargeting, dynamic retargeting for e-commerce
- **Exercise:** Design a 30-day retargeting sequence with specific creative and copy for each window

### Conversion Funnels
- **Framework:** Funnel selection matrix (business model × price point → funnel type)
- **Key content:** Common funnel types (lead magnet, webinar, VSL, booking, tripwire), conversion benchmarks per stage, funnel mapping technique, multi-step form strategy
- **Exercise:** Map your current funnel end-to-end with conversion rates at each step

### Landing Page Optimisation
- **Framework:** The 5-second test (headline, subhead, CTA, trust, visual — all visible above fold)
- **Key content:** Form field reduction, page speed benchmarks, mobile-first layout, trust elements (testimonials, logos, guarantees), CTA placement and repetition, heatmap analysis interpretation
- **Exercise:** Run the 5-second test on your current landing page with 3 people

### Landing Page Copywriting
- **Framework:** The PASTOR framework (Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response)
- **Key content:** Headline formulas (how-to, question, number, testimonial, direct), subhead strategy, bullet point writing, objection-handling sections, CTA copy (action verb + benefit)
- **Exercise:** Rewrite your landing page headline using 3 different formulas

### Nurture Sequences
- **Framework:** The 3-sequence system (welcome → nurture → re-engagement)
- **Key content:** Email timing (welcome: immediate + days 1,2,3,5,7; nurture: 2-3x/week; re-engagement: 60-90 day trigger), content types per stage (value, social proof, objection handling, direct offer), segmentation triggers
- **Exercise:** Outline a 7-email welcome sequence with subject lines and one-sentence content summaries

### Show Rates & Sales Process
- **Framework:** The confirmation stack (immediate confirm + 24hr reminder + 1hr reminder + post-no-show)
- **Key content:** Pre-call content (what to send between booking and call), SMS vs email for reminders, calendar embed strategies, sales script structures, objection handling frameworks
- **Exercise:** Write your 4-message confirmation stack with exact timing and content

### CRM & Pipeline Management
- **Framework:** The 5-stage pipeline (new lead → contacted → qualified → proposal → closed)
- **Key content:** Lead scoring criteria (engagement + fit), pipeline stage definitions, follow-up automation rules per stage, stale deal management, team assignment logic
- **Exercise:** Define your pipeline stages with specific entry/exit criteria for each

### A/B Testing
- **Framework:** The testing hierarchy (test in order: offer → headline → creative → audience → page elements)
- **Key content:** Sample size calculator usage, statistical significance thresholds (95%), testing one variable at a time, interpreting results (winner by conversion rate AND volume), iteration loops
- **Exercise:** Design 3 A/B tests for your highest-traffic page, prioritised by expected impact

### Lead Quality Tracking
- **Framework:** The quality feedback loop (score leads → track to sale → feed back to ad platform)
- **Key content:** Lead scoring models, cost per qualified lead vs cost per lead, quality-weighted ROAS, offline conversion tracking, CRM-to-ad-platform integration
- **Exercise:** Create a lead scoring rubric with 5 criteria and point values

### Reporting & KPI Dashboards
- **Framework:** The three-view dashboard (daily snapshot, weekly trend, monthly P&L)
- **Key content:** Metrics by funnel stage with benchmarks, weekly reporting cadence (pull → flag → decide → execute), when to act vs wait decision framework, conditional formatting for above/below target
- **Exercise:** Build a Google Sheet dashboard with the three-tab structure and fill in last week's data

### Budget & Unit Economics
- **Framework:** The backwards budget (revenue target → sales → calls → bookings → leads → budget)
- **Key content:** CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio (target 3:1+), breakeven ROAS formula, maximum allowable CPL, scaling rules (20% increase per 3-5 days), blended vs in-platform ROAS
- **Exercise:** Calculate your unit economics: LTV, max CPL, breakeven ROAS, and daily budget from revenue target

### Offer Creation & Positioning
- **Framework:** The 5-layer offer stack (core outcome → vehicle → bonuses → guarantee → price anchor)
- **Key content:** Four positioning lanes (price leader, niche specialist, premium/DFY, category creator), value stacking on landing pages, proprietary naming, guarantee types
- **Exercise:** Build your offer stack and choose a positioning lane

### Post-Sale & Referral Loop
- **Framework:** The 3-trigger referral system (after first win → at retention milestone → at renewal)
- **Key content:** 7-day onboarding sequence, referral incentive structures (two-sided vs status-based), review generation timing and prompts, upsell path mapping (entry → core → premium)
- **Exercise:** Write your onboarding sequence and identify your three referral trigger moments

### Email Deliverability
- **Framework:** The authentication stack (SPF + DKIM + DMARC)
- **Key content:** 14-day warm-up schedule with daily volumes, list hygiene practices (bounce removal, sunset policy, verification services), content signals for primary inbox (plain text vs HTML, link count, trigger words), double opt-in
- **Exercise:** Audit your authentication records, check your mail-tester.com score, clean your list

### Ad Policy & Compliance
- **Framework:** The 5 rejection triggers (personal attributes, before/after, income claims, sensational content, landing page mismatch)
- **Key content:** Meta and Google specific policies, compliant copy rewrites (personal callout → general framing), account health management, appeal process, building reputation on new accounts
- **Exercise:** Policy-proof your 3 best-performing ads by identifying and rewriting flaggable copy

### Video & Image Production
- **Framework:** The production stack (scripting → filming → editing → formatting)
- **Key content:** Equipment recommendations by budget tier, lighting and audio basics, editing software options (CapCut, DaVinci, Canva), platform format specifications (aspect ratios, lengths, safe zones), batch production workflows
- **Exercise:** Film and edit one ad using your phone, a ring light, and free editing software
