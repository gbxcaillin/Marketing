# The 24-Skill Marketing Framework

Each skill below includes: what it covers, what "strong" looks like in collateral, and the threshold for "partial" vs "missing."

## Stage 1: Who

### 1. Customer Avatar & Psychographics
**Covers:** Ideal customer profiling, demographics, psychographics, pain points, desires, objections, buying triggers, awareness levels.

- **Strong:** Teaches a structured avatar-building process with psychographic depth (fears, desires, identity). Includes frameworks for segmenting by awareness level or buyer stage. Goes beyond demographics into emotional drivers.
- **Partial:** Covers demographics and basic pain points but lacks psychographic depth. Or covers avatars without connecting them to ad targeting or messaging.
- **Missing:** No structured approach to defining who the customer is.

---

## Stage 2: What to Say

### 2. Ad Content Creation
**Covers:** Creating ad creative — video scripts, image ads, carousel formats, UGC frameworks, hook structures, content repurposing.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific creative frameworks (hook/story/offer, 3-step video script, testimonial formats). Covers multiple ad formats. Includes examples or templates.
- **Partial:** Covers one format well but ignores others. Or discusses creative principles without actionable frameworks.
- **Missing:** No guidance on how to create ad content.

### 3. Copywriting (Meta & Google)
**Covers:** Writing ad copy for paid platforms — headlines, descriptions, CTAs, platform-specific formats, character limits, responsive search ads.

- **Strong:** Teaches copy frameworks for both Meta (primary text, headlines) and Google (RSA structure, extensions). Covers persuasion principles applied to short-form ad copy.
- **Partial:** Covers one platform but not the other. Or teaches general copywriting without platform-specific application.
- **Missing:** No ad copywriting instruction.

### 4. Creative Diversity & Testing
**Covers:** Running multiple creative variations simultaneously, creative fatigue management, diversity strategies, algorithmic advantages of variety.

- **Strong:** Explains why creative diversity matters algorithmically (e.g., Meta's Andromeda system). Teaches how many variations to run, when to refresh, and how to test systematically.
- **Partial:** Mentions the need for multiple creatives without explaining the system behind it.
- **Missing:** No discussion of creative rotation, fatigue, or diversity.

### 5. Neuromarketing & Persuasion
**Covers:** Psychology-based marketing — cognitive biases, emotional triggers, social proof mechanics, urgency/scarcity, framing effects, resistance reduction.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific psychological principles with marketing applications. Goes beyond "use social proof" into mechanisms (e.g., physical vs psychological resistance, green brain vs logical brain, loss aversion framing).
- **Partial:** Mentions psychological principles without teaching how to apply them systematically.
- **Missing:** No psychology-based marketing instruction.

---

## Stage 3: Where to Run It

### 6. Ad Targeting & Audiences
**Covers:** Building audiences — custom audiences, lookalikes, interest targeting, exclusions, data hierarchy, audience sizing, warm vs cold segmentation.

- **Strong:** Teaches audience building with a data hierarchy (customers > leads > engagers > interests). Covers custom audience creation, lookalike strategies, and exclusion logic. Addresses audience sizing and overlap.
- **Partial:** Covers basic targeting (interests, demographics) without custom/lookalike strategy or data hierarchy.
- **Missing:** No audience-building instruction.

### 7. Campaign Structure & Account Setup
**Covers:** Account hierarchy (campaign > ad set > ad), naming conventions, CBO vs ABO, campaign types, budget allocation across campaigns, account organisation at scale.

- **Strong:** Teaches the full hierarchy with naming conventions. Explains when to use CBO vs ABO. Covers budget allocation rules (e.g., 70/20/10). Addresses scaling structure.
- **Partial:** Mentions campaign types without teaching organisation or naming. Or covers one platform's structure but not the other.
- **Missing:** No instruction on how campaigns are structured or organised.

### 8. Google Search Campaigns
**Covers:** Search campaign types — brand, competitor, non-brand, long-tail, shopping. Keyword strategy, match types, negative keywords, quality score, search terms report.

- **Strong:** Teaches multiple search campaign types with keyword strategy for each. Covers match types, negatives, and quality score optimisation.
- **Partial:** Covers brand campaigns only, or mentions search without teaching keyword strategy. Covers Google Ads experiments without core search mechanics.
- **Missing:** No Google Search instruction.

### 9. Retargeting
**Covers:** Retargeting strategy — sequential messaging, audience windows, frequency capping, retargeting creative, cross-platform retargeting, dynamic retargeting.

- **Strong:** Teaches retargeting as a system with sequential messaging (what to show at 1 day, 7 days, 30 days). Covers creative strategy for warm audiences specifically.
- **Partial:** Covers retargeting audiences (how to build them) without creative strategy or sequencing. Or mentions retargeting as part of a broader funnel discussion.
- **Missing:** No retargeting-specific instruction.

---

## Stage 4: Convert

### 10. Conversion Funnels
**Covers:** Funnel architecture — lead magnets, tripwires, webinars, VSL funnels, booking funnels, multi-step forms, funnel mapping, conversion rate benchmarks.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific funnel types with conversion benchmarks. Maps the full journey from ad click to sale. Covers different funnel architectures for different business models.
- **Partial:** Discusses funnels conceptually without teaching specific architectures or benchmarks.
- **Missing:** No funnel instruction.

### 11. Landing Page Optimisation
**Covers:** Page layout, above-the-fold design, form design, page speed, mobile optimisation, trust elements, CTA placement, heatmap analysis.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific layout principles with examples. Covers technical performance (speed, mobile). Addresses trust elements and CTA optimisation.
- **Partial:** Covers layout without technical performance, or vice versa. Or discusses landing pages only within a broader funnel context.
- **Missing:** No landing page instruction.

### 12. Landing Page Copywriting
**Covers:** Writing landing page copy — headline formulas, subheadline strategy, body copy structure, benefit vs feature framing, objection handling on-page, CTA copy.

- **Strong:** Teaches headline formulas, copy structure patterns, and how to handle objections within page copy. Separate from ad copywriting.
- **Partial:** Landing page discussion focuses on layout/design but not the persuasion copy itself. Or teaches copywriting generally without landing-page-specific application.
- **Missing:** No landing page copy instruction distinct from ad copy or general copywriting.

---

## Stage 5: Nurture & Close

### 13. Nurture Sequences
**Covers:** Email/SMS sequences post-opt-in — sequence structure, timing, content types, segmentation triggers, re-engagement, automation logic.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific sequence structures (welcome, nurture, re-engagement) with timing and content guidance. Covers segmentation triggers (what to send based on behaviour).
- **Partial:** Discusses follow-up generally without specific sequence structures or timing.
- **Missing:** No nurture sequence instruction.

### 14. Show Rates & Sales Process
**Covers:** Getting booked leads to show up — confirmation sequences, reminder cadence, pre-call content, objection pre-handling, sales scripts, close techniques.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific tactics for improving show rates (confirmation emails, SMS reminders, pre-call videos). May include sales scripts or close frameworks.
- **Partial:** Mentions show rates without specific improvement tactics. Or covers sales scripts without the pre-call nurture that drives attendance.
- **Missing:** No show rate or sales process instruction.

### 15. CRM & Pipeline Management
**Covers:** CRM setup, pipeline stages, lead scoring, deal tracking, follow-up automation, team workflows, reporting within CRM.

- **Strong:** Teaches CRM pipeline setup with defined stages, lead scoring criteria, and follow-up automation rules.
- **Partial:** Uses a CRM as part of other instruction (e.g., tracking leads) without teaching pipeline setup or lead scoring.
- **Missing:** No CRM or pipeline instruction.

### 16. A/B Testing
**Covers:** Testing methodology — what to test, test design, sample size, statistical significance, interpreting results, iterating on winners.

- **Strong:** Teaches what to test and in what order. Covers sample size requirements and significance thresholds. Explains how to iterate on results.
- **Partial:** Mentions testing without covering methodology (significance, sample size). Or covers platform-specific testing features without testing principles.
- **Missing:** No A/B testing instruction.

---

## Stage 6: Measure

### 17. Lead Quality Tracking
**Covers:** Tracking lead quality beyond volume — lead scoring, source attribution, cost per qualified lead, quality feedback loops to ad platforms.

- **Strong:** Teaches how to score leads by quality, track cost per qualified lead (not just CPL), and feed quality data back into ad platforms for optimisation.
- **Partial:** Tracks lead volume and CPL without quality differentiation. Or discusses quality conceptually without tracking mechanisms.
- **Missing:** No lead quality tracking instruction.

### 18. Reporting & KPI Dashboards
**Covers:** Building reporting systems — what to track at each funnel stage, reporting cadence, dashboard setup, when to act vs wait, benchmarking.

- **Strong:** Teaches metrics by funnel stage with benchmarks. Covers reporting cadence and decision frameworks (when data is actionable vs noise).
- **Partial:** Mentions metrics without a systematic reporting framework. Or tracks individual metrics without connecting them into a dashboard or cadence.
- **Missing:** No reporting system instruction.

### 19. Budget & Unit Economics
**Covers:** Financial metrics — CAC, LTV, breakeven ROAS, budget calculation, scaling rules, margin analysis, working backwards from revenue targets.

- **Strong:** Teaches CAC, LTV, and breakeven ROAS calculation. Shows how to work backwards from revenue targets to required ad spend. Covers scaling budget rules.
- **Partial:** Mentions budget without teaching the formulas. Or covers one metric (e.g., ROAS) without the full economics picture.
- **Missing:** No budget or unit economics instruction.

---

## Stage 7: Grow

### 20. Offer Creation & Positioning
**Covers:** Structuring the offer — pricing strategy, value stacking, bonuses, guarantees, positioning against competitors, naming, packaging.

- **Strong:** Teaches a structured offer-creation process (outcome, vehicle, bonuses, guarantee, pricing). Covers positioning lanes and competitive differentiation.
- **Partial:** Discusses offers in passing without a creation framework. Or covers pricing without positioning.
- **Missing:** No offer creation instruction. The collateral assumes the offer already exists.

### 21. Post-Sale & Referral Loop
**Covers:** Post-purchase systems — onboarding sequences, referral programs, review generation, upsell/cross-sell paths, retention metrics, customer success.

- **Strong:** Teaches post-sale onboarding, referral systems with specific triggers, and upsell paths. Treats post-sale as a marketing function, not just operations.
- **Partial:** Mentions retention or referrals without systematic frameworks. Or covers upsells without onboarding or referral mechanics.
- **Missing:** No post-sale instruction. The funnel ends at the sale.

### 22. Email Deliverability
**Covers:** Email infrastructure — domain authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), IP warm-up, list hygiene, inbox placement, content signals, sender reputation.

- **Strong:** Teaches domain authentication setup, warm-up schedules, and list hygiene practices. Covers content signals that affect inbox placement.
- **Partial:** Teaches email content/sequences without deliverability infrastructure. Or mentions "make sure emails land" without teaching how.
- **Missing:** No deliverability instruction.

### 23. Ad Policy & Compliance
**Covers:** Platform ad policies — Meta and Google restrictions, common rejection triggers, compliant copy strategies, appeal processes, account health management.

- **Strong:** Teaches specific policy areas (personal attributes, income claims, before/after, health restrictions). Shows how to write compliant copy that still converts. Covers account health.
- **Partial:** Warns about policies without teaching specific rules or compliant alternatives.
- **Missing:** No policy or compliance instruction.

### 24. Video & Image Production
**Covers:** Producing ad creative assets — filming techniques, lighting, editing software, image design, thumbnail creation, format specifications, asset management.

- **Strong:** Teaches production skills: how to film, edit, and design ad creative. Covers equipment, software, and format specs per platform.
- **Partial:** Teaches video scripting (what to say) without production skills (how to film/edit). Or covers one medium without the other.
- **Missing:** No production instruction.
